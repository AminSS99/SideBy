# SideBy iOS premium redesign — round 2

The second round responds to the feedback that the first concepts felt obvious and insufficiently premium.

## Removed from the design language

- Oversized editorial hero typography
- Neon A/B outlines and glowing controls
- Saturated orange gradient buttons
- Icon circles, badges, nested cards, and generic AI-dashboard styling
- Decorative glassmorphism and excessive rounded containers

## Directions

1. `01-quiet-ledger.png` — dark, precise ledger layout with an off-white primary action and minimal A/B accents.
2. `02-paper-intelligence.png` — warm editorial light mode with a strict baseline grid and print-inspired hierarchy.
3. `03-black-titanium.png` — tactile monochrome instrument with a split comparison field and restrained hardware material cues.

## Prompt set

All three were generated with the built-in ImageGen workflow at a 390 x 844 iOS aspect ratio. The current SideBy mobile screenshot was attached as the brand reference, and the rejected first-round home mock was attached as a negative reference.

- **Quiet Ledger:** Understated dark decision instrument; two-row ledger composer, sparse copper/cyan identifiers, off-white CTA, simple recent list, and minimal navigation.
- **Paper Intelligence:** Premium light-mode research journal; warm mineral paper, Swiss/product typography, baseline form rows, black CTA, and text-only decision history.
- **Black Titanium:** Precision-hardware direction; split A/B field on the base surface, smoked graphite materials, low-profile research bar, compact active-run status, and a thin metal dock.
