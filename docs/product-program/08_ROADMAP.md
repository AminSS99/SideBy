## Twelve-month themes and 25-candidate slates

The delivery objective is at least 25 genuinely new features/month. Each month maintains at least 30 candidate capabilities and an estimated 25-feature delivery slate; neither a plan nor a target is a shipped result. Suggested slate: 10 decision workflow, 5 collaboration/ecosystem, 5 mobile productivity, 3 trust/data portability, 2 experimental capabilities. Reliability/design/security fixes have a separate lane and do not count as new features. Publish admitted/shipped/deferred counts and reasons.

The thirty features seed discovery; detailed monthly slates must be written after interviews, current-feature audit and estimates. Do not invent 300 apparently unique features now to fill a table. If measured throughput supports fewer than 25 launches/month, report the capacity gap and propose staffing or smaller scope without weakening security gates.

## Annual and long-range planning

Year 1: reliable decision tool and verified ecosystem benefits. Year 2: repeated decision/outcome learning and selected team/vertical expansion if retention supports it. Years 3–5: trustworthy shared decision records and interoperable evidence workflows, contingent on economics and governance.

Years 10–20 are scenarios, not a backlog: personal decision memory controlled by the user; verifiable evidence exchange across agents; portable organizational decision records; local/private research and changing interaction hardware. Reassess annually against regulation, user control, model economics and actual demand. Avoid committing to a framework, vendor or speculative autonomous decision authority for twenty years.

## Weekly operating rhythm

Monday: customer/support evidence, incidents, cohort dashboard and next admitted work. Midweek: prototype review on physical mobile plus desktop, contract review with Core/native coordinator. Friday: release evidence, regressions, cost/trust review and roadmap update. After every execution update ledger, current risks and next batch; after each month update the next twelve months and revisit long-range assumptions. This is a workflow proposal, not a scheduled automation.
