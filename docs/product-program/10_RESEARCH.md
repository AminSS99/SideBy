# Research and competitive context

Research date: 2026-10-27. This is a small primary-source baseline, not exhaustive market validation or proof that any proposed feature is globally unique.

## Tools and coverage

Exa searches: official Perplexity project/research features, Elicit evidence workflows, and component-system documentation; 5 requested results each, plus a WebKit push query (20 requested results in the successful calls). Results were filtered for primary sources; duplicate Elicit routes and older Perplexity terminology were not counted as independent capabilities. TinyFish searched GSAP React guidance and official React Bits references; one multi-domain search returned no matches and was narrowed successfully. A TinyFish content extraction call failed schema validation; its content was not used. Exa initial schema errors were corrected using the connector's required objective field.

You.com was explicitly requested but no callable You.com tool appeared in the session inventory. No You.com-derived finding is claimed. No paid TinyFish browser automation or competitor account signup was performed.

Vendor pages describe their own products; their accuracy/superiority claims are not independently validated here. Avoid hardcoding transient corpus counts, pricing, model lists or component counts into SideBy marketing. Verify component licenses at adoption time.

## Differentiation hypothesis

A contextual, inspectable decision record that evolves with new evidence and real outcomes can be a stronger focus than another general research chat. Sensitivity, evidence independence, regret, change impact and ecosystem follow-through are candidate combinations. Competitors may already offer parts; test head-to-head tasks before advertising exclusivity.

## Next research protocol

Choose a specific user/job and evaluate SideBy plus three current alternatives using the same non-sensitive decision prompt, constraints and assessment rubric. Capture evidence quality, assumptions, changing priorities, mobile effort, cost and saved-decision continuity. Do not bypass competitor auth, automation restrictions or access controls.

Interview target users about their last real decision, not abstract enthusiasm. Record willingness to pay with an actual bounded offer, switching friction and privacy concerns. Refresh competitive notes quarterly and before public uniqueness/pricing claims.
